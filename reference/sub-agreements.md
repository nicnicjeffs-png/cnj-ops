# Subcontractor Agreements A & B: Key Terms

Summary of the CNJ subcontract templates. Use this for cash-flow timing, holdback tracking and PO/billing checks.
The signed templates (.docx) live outside this repo. This file summarizes them; it doesn't replace them.

Last reviewed: 2026-11-10 (from the Nov 2 template copies).

## Which agreement applies
| Agreement | Template | Use for |
|---|---|---|
| A (large value) | TEMP-OPS-006 | Subcontracts of $40,000 or more per job (net of HST) |
| B (small value) | TEMP-OPS-007 | Subcontracts under $40,000 per job (net of HST) |

The $40K threshold is a CNJ rule. It is not written in either template.

## Payment stages (% of approved subcontract / PO value)
| Stage | A | B | When payable |
|---|---|---|---|
| Start of work | 20% | 20% | Within 14 days of the sub starting the PO scope |
| Rough-in inspection passed or 50% complete | — | 30% | Within 14 days (B only) |
| Substantial completion (all PO work done + required final inspections passed) | 70% | 40% | Within 14 days |
| Holdback | 10% | 10% | Up to 60 days after the substantial-completion payment |
| **Total** | **100%** | **100%** | |

"Unless otherwise agreed to in writing by the Contractor." Any sub on different terms needs that in writing and noted on the PO.

## Holdback
- CNJ may retain the statutory 10% holdback under the NB **Construction Remedies Act**.
- Released after the holdback period expires, provided the legislative requirements are met and there are no valid claims against it.
- Confirm current holdback rules and timing with the accountant/lawyer before relying on them.

## No PO, no pay
- The sub gives a written estimate. Work is authorized only once CNJ accepts it and issues a PO.
- No work starts without an approved PO. Time-and-material work also needs a PO first.
- Changes or extras: the sub gives a written estimate, the customer approves, and CNJ issues a new PO before the work proceeds.
- Work done without a PO is unauthorized, and CNJ may refuse to pay for it.

## Invoicing requirements
- Submitted weekly, emailed to cnj@cnjbuilders.com.
- Must show: total amount, labour hours breakdown, itemized materials, description of the work done in the period, and receipts if requested.

## Compliance (before starting)
- Commercial General Liability insurance, minimum $2,000,000.
- Workers' compensation as required by law, with a valid WorkSafeNB clearance certificate. Coverage must stay in good standing for the whole project; CNJ can request updated certificates at any time.
- Any other insurance CNJ reasonably requires.

## Other terms
- Non-solicit: no work for CNJ clients for 2 years unless arranged through or agreed by CNJ.
- Follow CNJ's code of conduct on CNJ sites.
- Keep Raken updated on significant progress; give project updates when CNJ asks.
- Customer change requests go to the CNJ project manager. The sub does no out-of-scope work without approval and a PO.
- Termination for cause (poor or non-compliant work, uncorrected deficiencies, safety or conduct violations, delays, abandonment, lapsed insurance/WCB, material breach). CNJ may bring in others to finish, back-charge the sub, and withhold payment for defective, incomplete or disputed work.
- Return of CNJ property on termination; confidentiality survives termination.

## What this means for the books
- Every sub bill should match an approved PO. A bill with no PO is a flag (audit check).
- Track holdback as a payable on every sub bill (open audit finding #6).
- Cash-flow timing: start + 14 days, rough-in + 14 days (B), SC + 14 days, holdback ~60 days after the SC payment.
