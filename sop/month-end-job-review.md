# SOP — Month-End Job Review

**When:** First week after month-end, once bills and payroll for the month are entered
**Owner:** Nic (Claude prepares, Nic reviews with Chris)

## Steps (for each active job)
1. **Budget vs actual:** Compare JT budget vs actual to the QBO project P&L. Explain any difference over $500 (missing bill, wrong job, unmapped cost code, timing).
2. **Labour check:** Compare labour hours in QBO/JT to busybusy. Labour cost should be $30/hr with no markup.
3. **KPI:** Job profit ÷ direct labour cost.
   - ≥ 250%: on target
   - 180–250%: watch
   - < 180%: flag, find the cause, decide an action
4. **Change orders:** All approved COs are billed? Any unapproved CO work already done?
5. **Holdback:** Holdback held on sub bills and invoices is tracked and matches the agreements.
6. **Uncategorized costs:** JT cost mapping dashboard = 0.

## Output (Claude)
- Job table: Job | Contract $ | Billed | Cost to date | Profit | Labour $ | KPI % | Status
- Top 3 issues across all jobs with a recommended fix
- No changes made without Nic's approval
